The project's validated gesture gate: peak |linear accel| ≥ 0.5 g per 40 ms window, computed from the stream the chip already sends. Fires on taps, drums, shakes, pick-ups; silent on ambient. This is what the duck installations actually use instead of the on-chip detectors.
